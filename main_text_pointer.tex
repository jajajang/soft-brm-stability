Appendix~\ref{app:experiments} illustrates Theorems~\ref{thm:sgda_stability_noA7}
and~\ref{thm:pop_excess_risk} on a small MDP with two-layer networks of width $8$--$32$. Both the argument
stability and the excess population MSBE of the SGDA output decay as $n^{-1}$ (fitted slopes between
$-1.08$ and $-0.97$), for i.i.d.\ data and for a single trajectory, while the
constants implied by the theorems are loose.
